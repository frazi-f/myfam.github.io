About Us

A Story of Love & Unity

We are a happy family of six living in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, bound by love, respect, and shared dreams.

Our Journey Together

Our family began with the marriage of Gemechu Temesgen and Yemiserach Yohannes. Together, we have built a home filled with laughter, learning, and unconditional love.

We are blessed with four beautiful children — two sons and two daughters — each bringing their own unique joy and personality to our family. We believe that education, respect, and faith are the pillars that hold a family together.

Every day we strive to grow together, support one another, and create memories that will last a lifetime.
